### SRUTI-006: The golden-example eval script
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** a1ff190 · **GitHub:** #6

### SRUTI-007: The run: settle the piece prompt and the glossary form
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** 1bb27ed · **GitHub:** #7
**Gates:** [ ] lint (n/a; ruff clean) · [x] eval self-test 8/8

- **Runs:** five live runs, both glossary forms, before and after the fixes.
- **Final run:** hints glossary, 15/15 pieces pass, 1.5–4.1 s, ~1.44K / 0.41K tokens, about $0.0026 per
  piece.
- **Glossary:** contest and POTA conventions added to both forms, plus the fragment rule.
- **Prompt:** three rules added:
  - never offer a call sign that isn't in the text;
  - a rebuild that changes nothing is `none`;
  - a joined call sign keeps its `(?)` in the message.
- **Decision:** the hints file — equal on substance, it follows the fragment rule that the full form
  broke, at half the input tokens.
- **Where it's written:** ARCHITECTURE §The two tiers (the prompt verbatim, the user-message layout, the
  schema, the figures) and `poc/RESULTS.md` §v0.2.
- **Eval tooling:** the eval now saves raw answers and re-scores them offline (`--rescore`); three
  checker false alarms were fixed.
