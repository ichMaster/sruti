# v0.1 — Issues

Issues for phase **v0.1 — Text from the receiver, decoded on the Mac** (version **v0 — Groundwork: text
from the receiver's audio, the piece model proven**), derived from the Tasks and DoD in
[ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) §v0.1 and the contracts in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md). This file
covers one phase; ids start the numbering (**SRUTI-001…004**).

> **⟳ Reconciled (2026-10-03):** the phase was planned around the receiver's `CW_decoder` extension. On the
> v0.1 receivers it was either not offered or attached without ever producing text, so the owner decided
> that sruti decodes on the Mac with its own decoder. SRUTI-004 (the prototype decoder and the audio spike)
> was added; SRUTI-002 now records WAV + capture and decodes it on the Mac, with no tone offset to find;
> SRUTI-003 inventories the audio channel's messages. Reason: ROADMAP §v0.1 and ARCHITECTURE §The receiver,
> §The decoder, as rewritten.

The phase proves that CW from a public KiwiSDR's audio decodes into text on the Mac, settles
`kiwiclient`'s license, and records the sessions every later test replays. It builds on nothing; the
receiver and decoder modules, the fake receiver and the Python project are v1.1–v1.2, golden examples
are v0.2.

**What needs to be done:**

- **The spike** `poc/receiver/cw_spike.py`, standard library plus `kiwiclient`:
  - arguments `--receiver host:port`, `--freq <kHz>`, `--pboff <Hz>` (default 500), `--out <file.jsonl>`,
    `--replay <file.jsonl>`;
  - one connection, identified as `sruti`; sends only the documented `SET` messages;
  - prints the URI-decoded `cw_chars` as they arrive, plus `cw_wpm` and `cw_train` on a status line;
  - writes every message in both directions to the capture, one line each;
  - reports "receiver busy" and "time limit reached" and exits cleanly; Ctrl-C disconnects;
  - `--replay` prints the text of a saved capture and never connects.
- **The raw-capture format** — one JSON object per line, `{t, dir, msg}` — documented in ARCHITECTURE
  §The receiver. It is what v1.1's fake receiver replays.

### SRUTI-004 — Prototype CW decoder and the audio spike

**Description:**
sruti's own CW decoder, as a prototype: `poc/receiver/cw_decode.py` turns 12 kHz audio into text. The spike
`poc/receiver/cw_spike.py` gains what the decoder needs: it connects like the receiver's browser page,
tunes the channel to the signal's own frequency, records the uncompressed audio as WAV beside the capture,
and decodes the WAV on the Mac when the session ends.

**What needs to be done:**
- **The decoder** `poc/receiver/cw_decode.py`, numpy only, the six steps of ARCHITECTURE §The decoder:
  find the tone in 300–1000 Hz (90th-percentile power, at least 6 dB above the passband); follow its
  envelope (mix to 0 Hz, 12 ms smoothing, 5 ms hops, dB); key on an adaptive threshold (midway between the
  25th and 97th percentile over 4 s, ±1 dB hysteresis, no signal under 14 dB of contrast); run lengths with
  glitches folded; the dot length from two clusters of mark lengths, re-estimated over the last 24 marks;
  the Morse table with prosigns, `[err]` for an unknown code.
- **Its command line:** `cw_decode.py <file.wav>` prints the text, the tone, the speed and the duration;
  `--selftest` decodes synthetic CW — known text at several speeds and signal-to-noise ratios, band-limited
  to the passband — and an empty passband, which must decode to nothing.
- **The spike**, audio mode:
  - `--browser-path`: the connection timestamp from `GET /VER`, the `/ws/no_wf/<ts>/SND` path and the
    page's greeting, as ARCHITECTURE §The receiver describes;
  - `--freq` is the signal's own frequency — no passband-centre shift, which put the signal at ~1000 Hz,
    outside the passband;
  - `--audio`: `SET compression=0` and the samples written to a WAV beside the capture (ADPCM decoded if
    a receiver still sends it);
  - after the session, the WAV is decoded with `cw_decode` and the text printed; no audio → exit 1;
  - the signal level and the frame count are reported every 10 s; `MSG client_public_ip` is masked in the
    capture;
  - the receiver's extension stays behind `--kiwi-decoder`, off by default.
- **ARCHITECTURE:** §The receiver (connecting like the browser, the message table, frequency and tone, the
  extension not used, the capture with its WAV) and the new §The decoder.

**Dependencies:** SRUTI-001

**Expected result:**
One spike command records a public receiver's CW audio and prints the text sruti's own decoder reads from
it; the decoder passes its synthetic self-test offline.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [x] `cw_decode.py --selftest` passes: every synthetic text decodes exactly at 14–28 WPM down to 6 dB SNR,
      and the empty passband decodes to nothing. *(5/5 at close; 10/10 after the code review.)*
- [x] Reading the spike shows that in audio mode it sends only the documented tuning messages (ARCHITECTURE
      §The receiver table), over one connection named `sruti`.
- [x] An empty or missing WAV is reported, not a crash.
- [x] **Tests:** the self-test above (no automated suite before v1.1).
- [x] **Contract:** ARCHITECTURE §The receiver (the message table, the capture + WAV) and §The decoder
      (characters, prosigns as `<AR>`, `[err]`) describe what the prototype does; their pinning tests land
      with v1.1 (the fake receiver) and v1.2 (the decoder).
- [x] **Gates:** n/a — there is no `pyproject.toml` before v1.1; `ruff check poc/receiver` is clean.
