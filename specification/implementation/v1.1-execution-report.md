### SRUTI-010: The receiver link: connect, stream, reconnect, and the fake receiver
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** 93e8773 · **GitHub:** #10
**Gates:** [x] lint · [x] tests (62) · [x] CI

- **The link:** the async link over an injected transport, connecting like the browser page. It handles
  keepalive and AR OK, publishes the four event types, and moves through the states `connecting`,
  `listening`, `busy`, `reconnecting`, `time_limit`, `stopped` and `ended`.
- **Backoff:** exponential and capped, with ±25 % jitter; busy waits at least `busy_retry_s`.
- **Transport:** the real one runs on `websockets`.
- **The fake receiver:** replays WAV + capture in memory, with no socket. Replaying `cq` gives
  byte-identical audio and the capture's messages, also across a drop.
- **Time limit:** the time-limit signal names (`inactivity_timeout`, `ip_limit`) come from the KiwiSDR
  web client and have not been observed yet. The live hour didn't reach the receiver's 600-minute
  inactivity limit.

### SRUTI-011: `sruti listen`: headless run, recording, and the live hour
**Status:** completed · **Commit:** da94a4c · **GitHub:** #11
**Gates:** [x] lint · [x] tests (69) · [x] CI

- **The command:** `sruti listen` prints the states and the level every 10 s, with `--raw` and
  `--record <dir>` (WAV + capture, `<started>-<receiver>-<freq>`). There is a hidden `--drop-after`, and
  SIGINT stops cleanly.
- **ARCHITECTURE:** the directory edge is `http :80`; headless mode and the CLI flags are described.
  CLAUDE.md's CLI contract follows.
- **Live smoke test** (40 s, 18:29 UTC): the real transport connected at once.
  - All setup messages went out, and AR OK answered `audio_rate`.
  - About 23.5 frames per second arrived.
  - SIGINT left valid files, and a 486 Hz tone stood 20 dB above the noise, so the byte order is right.
- **The live hour** (Trémolat, 7027.5 kHz, 18:31–19:31 UTC):
  - 84,208 frames over 2 connections; the forced drop at 20 min reconnected in 5 s.
  - The audio flow was steady at 220–241 frames per 10 s, except about 5 s missed during the reconnect.
  - Exit 0, with only the documented messages sent.
- **Replay check:** the hour's recording replays through the fake receiver to identical audio
  (43,114,496 samples, 59.9 min) and identical received messages (475).

**Manual (owner):**

- The hour's recording replays to the same audio and messages — confirmed by owner (2026-10-04)
